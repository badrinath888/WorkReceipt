- WorkReceipt turns your Claude Code sessions and Git history into a report your client can read.

## Scene 2: overview
- Open WorkReceipt, and the Overview shows your projects, weekly session activity and exported reports at a glance.

## Scene 3: projects
- Pick a project and name your client.
- Then load its activity. WorkReceipt reads your sessions and Git history right here on your Mac.

## Scene 4: studio
- Report Studio lists the work you requested beside the Git commits that back it up.
- Every description is editable, so you can put it in words your client understands.

- Before anything is shared, the Privacy Center lists every masked value.
- Export stays locked until you review each high severity finding yourself.

## Scene 6: export
- Then export. WorkReceipt creates an HTML report and a PDF, both made on your computer.

- The finished report keeps work requested, changes delivered and recorded checks clearly apart.
- A request is never presented as proof of completion.
- Open the PDF, and your client has a clean, portable copy.

## Scene 8: outro
- WorkReceipt. A receipt for your AI-assisted work. Private by design, built for your Mac.
